# Earlier work and the professor's feedback

The team ("The Connectors") worked under the names PawPal and PawPop from Week 2 to Week 5. On 2026-10-20 the owner decided to restart the project from scratch, keeping only the premise of dogs and pets. This folder keeps the earlier work as evidence of the journey, which is itself material for the Week 7 midterm ("how it has impacted your semester project").

## Professor feedback (verbatim, as pasted by the owner)

First note:

> Hi Team - One thing [] said that struck with me was when he asked if you have talked to cat owners? You are trending app and platform, but I think there is a higher level opportunity here. I like where you are going with your community opportunities. I encourage you to not move towards an "app that can do it all" for dog owners and move towards what would be really meaningful for the pet community. You may have to have a few more interviews with dog walkers, caregivers, and yes, even cat owners to really unearth the thing you want to focus on. Will be waiting anxiously to see what you do next!

Second note:

> Hi Team - I think you've made real progress in identifying trust, local relevance, compatibility, and community as important needs. Still, the project feels broad. Choose a specific community context. Urban vs suburban matters and choose one primary service to prove first. You yourself call out your "empty at launch" threat and that's important because then you have to ask what creates value before the network is large? This leads me to planning an event experience and using digital tools to support that experience might be something to think about. That'll push the waveline beyond activity into experiential. Right now I can see what the user does, but not yet clearly how the person changes. What does your experience give them emotionally, socially, practically, or in terms of identity. Go for it!
