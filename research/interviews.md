# Team interviews (raw)

These are the team's real interviews, pasted by the owner on 2026-10-20 and kept here word for word (curly quotation marks became straight ones; nothing else changed, typos included). Part 1 is question-and-answer interviews. Part 2 is profile write-ups in the course's value format (functional, financial, emotional, identity, core meaning).

Known caveats (checked 2026-10-20, moderate-high confidence):
- The Part 1 script asks "If there were an app specifically for dog owners in your area...". That question invites app-shaped answers.
- Two pairs of Part 2 profiles look like the same person written up twice. The "Weiner dog group, $50/month insurance, travel" owner matches the "dachshund meetup, $50 insurance, travel care" owner. The "NYC student, multi-location drop-off membership, family pays insurance" profile matches the "NYC student with a male poodle, citywide drop-off membership, father pays insurance" profile. That makes about 11 distinct people, not 13.
- Few interviewees are confirmed New Yorkers. Ani lives in Yerevan and the couple lives in Washington, DC. Only the student (possibly counted twice) is confirmed to be in NYC.
